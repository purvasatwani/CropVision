How it works

  1. Capture

    Take a clear, close photo of one leaf in good light, or upload one you already have.

  2. Analyse on-device

    The leaf is resized and passed through the trained network. No image leaves your machine.

  3. Read the result

    You get the most likely condition, how sure the model is, and general care notes to discuss with an expert.
